# Original images (reference for the patches)

All patches are created against these images. `tools/build.py` and `tools/vorbereiten.py` check the SHA1
(skip with `--ohne-pruefsumme`).

| Disc | File name (expected in `ISO/`) | Size (bytes) | CRC32 | SHA1 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Xenosaga Episode III - Also sprach Zarathustra (USA) (Disc 1).iso | 3 923 804 160 | CFD0D722 | 30a887e6942cef57099bc9c84de5b39974e62f9a |
| 2 | Xenosaga Episode III - Also sprach Zarathustra (USA) (Disc 2).iso | 4 515 954 688 | 1855B442 | 9902a798f2f4a7d7a4af6078ec3e5ec5107d5450 |

If your dump differs, the tools may still work (the formats are the same), but patches made by others will not apply to it.
